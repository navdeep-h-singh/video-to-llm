# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Courtesy and patience, including toward people who are new to this, to Python,
  or to video tooling.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change without that being a
  judgement of you.
- Bug reports that describe what happened rather than how annoying it was.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub's **Report content** feature, or by
contacting the maintainers directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that breach this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe breaches.

## Scope

Applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages
— and anywhere someone is representing the project.
